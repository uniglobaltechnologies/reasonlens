## 3. Round One Draft Responses (Core)

Use these as the first draft for the application form. Adjust to exact word limits in the portal when the 2026-2027 form opens.

### Q1. What problem are you solving, and what challenge does your organization face in adopting technology to solve it?

Educators and education-serving nonprofits are under pressure to adopt AI quickly, but most lack practical governance, confidence, and safety testing capability. The result is fragmented adoption: some teams avoid AI entirely due to risk, while others deploy it without enough safeguards. Both outcomes reduce quality and equity for learners.

Our organization built ReasonLens to address this gap with a single platform for (1) AI readiness assessment across established international frameworks, (2) evidence-linked learning pathways for staff capability development, (3) policy drafting grounded in regulatory and framework context, and (4) practical AI safety audits for tools used in education.

Our technology adoption challenge is scale and consistency. We have validated the product architecture and workflows, but we need funding and cloud capacity to move from early implementation to broader, repeatable delivery across multiple institutions and partners. Specifically, we need to harden model governance, automate impact measurement, and support more concurrent users and institutional cohorts while maintaining privacy and reliability standards.

The problem is no longer whether AI will be used in education; it is whether institutions can use it responsibly, measurably, and equitably. Our mission is to give education organizations practical infrastructure for that transition.

### Q2. How will your proposed project tackle this challenge?

We propose the **ReasonLens Responsible AI Acceleration Program**: a 12-month project to scale our platform into a grant-supported, high-impact service for education-serving nonprofits and partner institutions.

The project has four delivery streams:

1. Platform scale and reliability
- Increase platform resilience and throughput for multi-institution cohorts.
- Improve observability and incident response for AI services and user-facing workflows.

2. Responsible generative AI controls
- Strengthen policy generation quality controls with traceable evidence references and prompt-level guardrails.
- Expand safety audit workflows with standardized risk dimensions and benchmark outputs.

3. Measurement and outcomes
- Build an impact analytics layer that tracks educator capability growth, policy completion, and safety-audit adoption.
- Produce partner-ready reporting for grant accountability and institutional decision-making.

4. Implementation support for partners
- Provide structured onboarding, role-based guidance, and rollout playbooks for education organizations.
- Establish a lightweight support model for institutions with lower AI maturity.

This project directly addresses our scaling challenge by combining product hardening, governance-by-design, and measurable service delivery.

### Q4. How likely is this project to accelerate your mission and solve your challenge?

This project is highly likely to accelerate mission delivery because it addresses our bottlenecks directly: scalable infrastructure, implementation standardization, and impact evidence.

Without this project, growth remains constrained to smaller cohorts and manual support effort. With grant support, we can move to repeatable implementation across multiple organizations while maintaining quality.

Mission acceleration outcomes include:
- Faster institutional adoption of responsible AI practice
- Better educator confidence and capability progression
- Improved policy quality and governance maturity
- More consistent use of practical AI safety evaluation before deployment

In short, the project turns a working platform into a mission-scale service model.

### Q5. If selected, how will you use unrestricted funds and AWS credits?

Unrestricted funds will be used to:
- Expand implementation capacity (program delivery, partner success, and quality assurance)
- Build and maintain impact evaluation and reporting
- Support product development for partner-facing features and governance tooling

AWS credits will be used to:

This mix allows us to fund people, process, and cloud infrastructure together, which is required for real mission impact.

## 4. Pathfinder-Specific Addendum (Use if requested in the 2026 form)

### Pathfinder prompt draft: What is your generative AI use case and why is it impactful?

ReasonLens uses generative AI to convert complex governance and capability challenges into practical outputs for education teams: policy drafts, role-aware implementation guidance, and conversational support tied to user context.

Impact comes from reducing the gap between AI strategy and day-to-day practice. Instead of static guidance, institutions receive actionable, contextual outputs aligned to their maturity and goals.

### Pathfinder prompt draft: How will you implement responsible AI?

Responsible AI controls include:
- Human-in-the-loop review for policy adoption and institutional decisions
- Prompt and output guardrails to avoid fabricated compliance claims
- Audit logs and traceability of generated outputs
- Data minimization and role-based access controls
- Structured safety testing of AI-supported workflows before broad rollout
- Ongoing monitoring for quality, bias, and failure patterns
